# Morse Trainer QT Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 14, 2026**

Morse Trainer QT respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

---

# 中文隐私政策

## 1. 概述

Morse Trainer QT 尊重并重视您的隐私。

本应用用于摩尔斯电码（CW）的学习与训练。我们尽量减少对用户数据的处理，并且不要求用户创建独立的 Morse Trainer QT 账户。

---

## 2. 学习与练习数据

Morse Trainer QT 会在您的设备上保存与学习和训练有关的数据，例如：

- 课程学习进度
- 练习记录
- 练习日期
- 训练成绩
- 与训练功能相关的设置和状态

这些数据用于提供应用的核心学习和训练功能。

---

## 3. iCloud 与 CloudKit 同步

如果您使用 iCloud 同步功能，Morse Trainer QT 可能通过 Apple 的 iCloud / CloudKit 服务同步支持的学习与练习数据。

这些数据存储在与您的 Apple iCloud 账户相关联的 CloudKit 私有数据库中。

Morse Trainer QT 不运营用于保存这些学习数据的独立云端服务器。

iCloud 和 CloudKit 服务由 Apple 提供，并受 Apple 相应的服务条款和隐私政策约束。

---

## 4. App 内购买

Morse Trainer QT 提供一次性非消耗型 App 内购买项目，用于解锁完整访问权限。

所有购买和付款均通过 Apple App Store 和 StoreKit 处理。

Morse Trainer QT 不直接接收、处理或保存您的：

- 信用卡信息
- 银行卡信息
- Apple Account 密码
- 其他支付凭证

购买资格由 Apple 的 StoreKit 系统验证和恢复。

---

## 5. 用户账户

Morse Trainer QT 不要求您注册独立的应用账户。

我们不会要求您向 Morse Trainer QT 提供用户名或密码以使用学习功能。

---

## 6. 广告与跟踪

Morse Trainer QT：

- 不包含第三方广告
- 不使用用户数据进行广告定向
- 不为了广告目的跨 App 或网站跟踪用户
- 不出售用户的个人数据

---

## 7. 第三方服务

Morse Trainer QT 的部分功能依赖 Apple 提供的系统服务，包括：

- Apple iCloud
- Apple CloudKit
- Apple App Store
- Apple StoreKit

这些服务的数据处理方式受 Apple 自身的隐私政策和相关条款约束。

---

## 8. 数据保留与删除

存储在设备本地的学习数据可能会在您删除应用时被删除。

通过 iCloud / CloudKit 同步的数据可能继续保存在您的 iCloud 环境中，并受 Apple 的 iCloud 数据管理机制约束。

重新安装应用或在其他兼容设备上使用同一 iCloud 账户时，部分学习进度可能会再次同步。

---

## 9. 数据安全

Morse Trainer QT 使用 Apple 提供的系统框架和服务处理 iCloud 同步及 App 内购买。

我们不会通过自建服务器收集或保存您的支付信息。

---

## 10. 儿童隐私

Morse Trainer QT 不会通过独立运营的服务器有意收集儿童的个人信息。

---

## 11. 隐私政策变更

如果 Morse Trainer QT 的功能、数据处理方式或所使用的服务发生变化，本隐私政策可能会相应更新。

更新后的版本将在此页面公布，并更新生效日期。

---

如果您对本隐私政策、数据处理方式或 Morse Trainer QT 有任何疑问，请联系：

**Email: phocircs@icloud.com**

# Privacy Policy

## 1. Overview

Morse Trainer QT respects your privacy.

The app is designed for learning and practicing Morse code (CW). We aim to minimize the processing of user information and do not require users to create a separate Morse Trainer QT account.

---

## 2. Learning and Practice Data

Morse Trainer QT stores information related to learning and practice on your device, which may include:

- Course progress
- Practice records
- Practice dates
- Training results
- Settings and states related to training features

This information is used to provide the app's core learning and training functionality.

---

## 3. iCloud and CloudKit Synchronization

If you use iCloud synchronization, Morse Trainer QT may synchronize supported learning and practice data through Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services.

This data is stored in a CloudKit private database associated with your Apple iCloud account.

Morse Trainer QT does not operate an independent cloud server for storing this learning data.

Apple provides the iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure, and use of these services is subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

---

## 4. In-App Purchases

Morse Trainer QT offers a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase that provides Full Access.

All purchases and payments are processed through the Apple App Store and StoreKit.

Morse Trainer QT does not directly receive, process, or store your:

- Credit card information
- Bank card information
- Apple Account password
- Other payment credentials

Purchase entitlement is verified and restored through Apple's StoreKit system.

---

## 5. User Accounts

Morse Trainer QT does not require you to create a separate in-app account.

You are not required to provide a username or password to Morse Trainer QT in order to use its learning features.

---

## 6. Advertising and Tracking

Morse Trainer QT:

- Does not contain third-party advertising
- Does not use user data for targeted advertising
- Does not track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes
- Does not sell users' personal data

---

## 7. Third-Party Services

Some Morse Trainer QT features rely on services provided by Apple, including:

- Apple iCloud
- Apple CloudKit
- Apple App Store
- Apple StoreKit

Information processed by these services is subject to Apple's own privacy policies and applicable terms.

---

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Learning data stored locally on your device may be removed when you delete the app.

Data synchronized through iCloud / CloudKit may remain within your iCloud environment and is subject to Apple's iCloud data management mechanisms.

If you reinstall the app or use another compatible device with the same iCloud account, supported learning progress may synchronize again.

---

## 9. Data Security

Morse Trainer QT uses Apple-provided system frameworks and services for iCloud synchronization and in-app purchases.

We do not collect or store your payment information on independently operated servers.

---

## 10. Children's Privacy

Morse Trainer QT does not knowingly collect children's personal information through independently operated servers.

---

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Morse Trainer QT's features, data practices, or supporting services change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

---

## 12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, data handling, or Morse Trainer QT, please contact:

**Email: phocircs@icloud.com**
